Why these 10 picks

  • Hero authenticity: real Brazilian brands (Lola, Inoar, Natura, Granado) carry the positioning and their own compliance. They need a distributor deal first.
  • Margin engine: CJ private-label body butter, mist and oil can carry your own label and higher margins, but only once their ingredient lists and documents are confirmed.
  • Order value: branded masks retail at $14–17 in the US, too low to carry ad costs alone. Sell them as a 3-mask routine or with an oil, and use the gift set to lift baskets.

Before listing any product, confirm

  • Exact supplier listing, variant and warehouse (US and/or EU)
  • Product cost, shipping cost and delivery time per country
  • Full INCI list exactly as on the label, net content, batch and expiry handling
  • For the EU: Responsible Person name and address, CPNP notification, safety report (CPSR)
  • Photos you're allowed to use (CJ listings or brand-supplied)
  • Return and damage policy of the supplier

Regulations

Compliance before selling

European Union

  • Every cosmetic needs a Responsible Person established in the EU and a CPNP notification before it is sold (Regulation 1223/2009).
  • A Product Information File with a safety report signed by a qualified assessor must exist and be kept for 10 years.
  • Shipping from outside the EU straight to EU customers usually makes you the importer, so you may become the Responsible Person unless one is appointed.
  • Benchmark cost for a safety report, PIF and CPNP: about £130–£465 per product (one provider's estimate).
  • Accessories aren't cosmetics, but EU product-safety rules (GPSR) require an EU-based economic operator for consumer products sold in the EU.

United States (MoCRA)

  • Businesses with under about $1 million average annual US cosmetic sales are exempt from facility registration and product listing, except for certain product types such as eye-area products.
  • The "responsible person" is the manufacturer, packer or distributor named on the label; with private label that may be you.
  • Labels still need a full ingredient declaration and a US contact for adverse-event reports; serious adverse events must be reported to the FDA.
  • Confirm your status with the FDA's MoCRA pages or a regulatory adviser before launch.

Claims to keep out of listings

Retail listings for some of these products use drug-like wording such as "hair growth", "anti-acne", "whitening" or "repairs damage permanently". Don't copy it. Use cosmetic wording such as "softens", "smooths the look of", "leaves hair feeling".

Your decisions

Approval checklist

  1. Payment provider. Choose one that works for a Montenegro business and supports several currencies (needed for USD in the US market).
  2. EU route. Either appoint an EU Responsible Person and notify each cosmetic on CPNP, or start the EU with branded products already on the EU market and accessories only.
  3. CJ. Log in, check each pick (listing, US/EU warehouse, cost, INCI, documents), list approved ones as drafts, turn on order and tracking sync, and decide on automatic payment.
  4. Brand distributors. Send the Lola from Rio US wholesale form and contact Natura US, Granado and an Inoar distributor about dropship terms and documents.
  5. Syncee (optional, paid): approve a plan if you want EU-based suppliers.
  6. Shipping rates. Set US and EU rates from supplier quotes. Your international zone is missing 13 EU countries (BG, HR, CY, EE, GR, HU, LV, LT, LU, MT, RO, SK, SI).
  7. Activate markets United States and European Union once 1–6 are done.
  8. Business details. Store name, legal entity, contact details and the draft policies (unchanged from before).
  9. Optional: allow wzdavt-r6.myshopify.com and cdn.shopify.com in this environment's network settings so I can take previews.
