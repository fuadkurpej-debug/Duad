# Skin & Beauty — importing CJ Dropshipping / Syncee products

The **Skin & Beauty** theme (unpublished) is set up so imported products look premium automatically.
These steps keep them that way.

## 1. Put each product in the right collection

Collections fill themselves. Give each imported product **one tag** (or set its product type) from this table:

| Tag (or product type)                         | Appears in                        |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| `cleanser` (Cleanser)                         | Facial Cleansers, Skincare        |
| `serum` (Serum)                               | Serums, Skincare                  |
| `moisturizer` (Moisturizer)                   | Moisturizers, Skincare            |
| `eye-care` (Eye Care)                         | Moisturizers, Skincare            |
| `face-mask` (Face Mask)                       | Face Masks, Skincare              |
| `toner`, `sunscreen`, `skincare`              | Skincare                          |
| `body-care` (Body Care)                       | Body Care, Body & Hair            |
| `hair-care` (Hair Care)                       | Hair Care, Body & Hair            |
| `lip-care` (Lip Care), `beauty-tool` (Beauty Tool) | Beauty Essentials, Beauty    |
| `makeup` (Makeup), `beauty`                   | Beauty                            |

- **New Arrivals** and **Best Sellers** include every active product automatically.
- Optional tags: `new` and `bestseller` show a small badge. Only use `bestseller` if it really sells best.
- `ships-separately` adds a note that the item may arrive in its own parcel.

Tip: CJ and Syncee both let you set tags and product type during import.

## 2. Supplier names are hidden automatically

Vendors like "CJ Dropshipping" or "Syncee" never appear on the store.
The list is in **Theme settings → Product cards → Hidden vendor names**.
To show the real brand, fill **Brand shown on store** (`custom.brand`) on the product page in admin.

## 3. Product fields (optional, in the product editor under Metafields)

Each one shows only when filled. Never copy claims you cannot verify.

- **Subtitle**: one short line under the title.
- **Key benefits**: 2–4 short lines. Cosmetic wording only, no "treats", "cures" or "whitening".
- **Full ingredient list (INCI)**: exactly as on the packaging. Leave it empty if the supplier can't provide it.
- **How to use**, **Suitable for**, **Texture**, **Size**.
- **Delivery estimate**: e.g. "Delivered in 7–12 business days". Use the supplier's real shipping time.
- **Pairs well with**: picks products for "Complete your ritual".
- **Lowest price in last 30 days**: needed in the EU when you show a sale price.

## 4. Before publishing each imported product

- Shorten the title (for example "Hydrating Vitamin C Serum 30 ml", not a 20-keyword supplier title).
- Remove supplier claims you can't back up, like "whitening", "anti-acne", "removes wrinkles" or "medical grade".
- Check the compare-at (old) price. Only keep it if the product really sold at that price.
- Keep 4–8 good images. Delete size charts and Chinese-text banners if they don't help.
- For white-background packshots: **Customize → Product page → Image fit → Show the whole image**.
  For cards: **Theme settings → Product cards → Image fit**.

## 5. When the real products are in

- Archive the 12 placeholder products. Archiving is reversible; it is not deletion.
- Menu links to empty collections are hidden on the live store automatically.
