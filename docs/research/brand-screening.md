# Brand-Name Conflict Screening: Home & Living brand (EN/SR)

**This is not legal advice.** It is a web-search screen only, run on 2026-10-21. It does not show that any name is free to use or register, and it does not show that any domain is available.

## Method and limits

- The only tool was WebSearch, which returns third-party summaries of pages. No pages were fetched directly. I ran no WHOIS or registrar checks and searched no official trademark database (USPTO, EUIPO eSearch/TMview, WIPO, or the Serbian, Montenegrin or BiH offices).
- **"Found"** below means a search result showed the item, with its URL. **"Uncertain"** means the result was thin, contradicted by another result, or could not be repeated.
- The trademark hits come from Justia, which mirrors USPTO data. It is not the official register, and a status shown there may be out of date.
- A search that finds nothing is weak evidence. Small shops, Instagram-only brands and registered marks are often not indexed.

## Findings by candidate

### 1. Kutak / Kutak Home: high conflict in your main market

**Found:**
- PlanPlus.rs lists **"Kutak – posuđe i kućni dekor"** at Svetogorska 30 (Stari Grad), Golsvordijeva 9 (Vračar) and Bulevar umetnosti 4 (Novi Beograd), Belgrade. That is the same product category you plan to sell.
- The company's site (kytak.rs, "Kytak" being Кутак written with Latin-looking letters) reportedly says **Nomen** has traded as Kutak for over 20 years in import, wholesale and retail. I saw this only through a search summary and did not read the page directly.
- **Dunjin kutak** is a home decor and gift shop in Novi Sad.
- Other businesses use "kutak" in their names: Mali Kutak (a therapy studio), Moj Kutak (design and events), Vukov božićni kutak (a Christmas concept store). The word works as an ordinary noun, so it is hard to own and hard to rank for in Serbian search.
- In US search, "Kutak" mostly returns **Kutak Rock LLP**, a large law firm (kutakrock.com). I found no indexed site at **kutak.com** and no Instagram handle "kutakhome" or "kutak_home". Neither of these shows the name is available.
- I found no EUIPO or US home-goods trademark for "Kutak". That search was not conclusive.

**Uncertain:**
- Whether Nomen holds a registered Serbian trademark for KUTAK. This is not verified, but a 20-year retail brand makes it plausible.
- Hungarian *kutak* = "wells", the plural of *kút*. Search results support this through usage. It is a neutral meaning, relevant to the Hungarian-speaking community in Vojvodina.

**Pronunciation:** native in Serbian (Кутак). English speakers may stress it unpredictably. No negative meanings found.

### 2. Stillhaus: crowded in interiors

**Found:**
- **Das Stilhaus**, Munich (Donnersbergerstraße 14, previously 44a): a furniture and interiors shop. Culture Trip lists it as "Stillhaus".
- **stilhaus**, Rothrist, Switzerland: a large showcase for building and furnishing brands (Architonic, Red Dot).
- **Still Haus Interiors** (San Antonio), **Stillhouse Design** (Philadelphia), **Still House Interiors** (Indiana).
- Other uses: the **Stillhaus** thermal spa in Columbus, OH (opening planned for 2026), **Stillhaus Co.** (property management, Austin), and @be.stillhaus (a lifestyle blog).
- Justia shows registered US marks for **STILLHOUSE** covering glass beverageware, pouring spouts and ice chests. These are close in sound and partly in the same goods classes.

**Uncertain:** who runs stillhaus.com. Search returned no content from that domain.

**Meaning:** German *still* + *Haus* means "quiet house". It reads German rather than Scandinavian.

### 3. Ivora Home

**Found:**
- **Ivora Lights** (ivoralights.com): lighting and home decor, Utah address.
- **Ivora living** (ivoraliving.myshopify.com): a home-decor shop selling vases, rugs and pillows. A later search could not find it again.
- **IVORI Home** (ivorihome.com): 3D wall panels.
- An "Ivora" rattan dining chair (bali & pari) sold at Home Depot.
- **Ivora Gouda** pottery, a historic name in vintage resale.
- ivora.eu exists, but what it sells is unclear.
- I found no exact US mark for IVORA (close marks: IVORIE, IVORINE).

**Meaning:** suggests ivory, which carries an ethical overtone, and sounds like a woman's name. That is a weaker fit for a gender-neutral brand.

### 4. Linden & Oak

**Found:**

- **Oak & Linen** (home goods), **Linen & Oak Candle Co.**, **Linden & London** (home fragrance), **Linden and Co.** candles on Amazon.
- Justia shows US marks **LINDEN MARKET** (furniture) and **LINDEN STREET** (Classes 20/21), and **LINDEN OAKS** (real estate).
- I found no exact LINDEN & OAK record.

**Pronunciation and meaning:** English-only, with nothing in Serbian ("lipa i hrast"). The ampersand makes domains and handles messy.

### 5. Oaklume: an invented name, but already in use

**Found:**

- latteaglobal.com sells an **"OakLume Nordic Semi Flush Mount Ceiling Light"** in its Scandinavian collection.
- A separate **"OakLume Studio"** nightstand was mentioned. This is uncertain and seen only in a summary.
- A GitHub repo "raiox-oaklume-luminarias" (*luminárias* is Portuguese for light fixtures). Its purpose is unclear.
- "oaklume" appears on a naming-generator list (namewisp) and as a website-template name (Temlis). Names that sound AI-generated tend to collide.

**Pronunciation:** unclear in English and awkward in Serbian. No negative meanings found.

### 6. Mirun Home

**Found:**
- Justia shows **MIRUN**, US Reg. No. 5641846, serial 87899283, registered Jan 1, 2019, **Class 21**. The goods listed include cosmetic brushes, toilet brushes, deep fryers and mouse traps, with first use claimed in 2012. That overlaps household goods. Its current status is uncertain: the Justia page may be out of date, and the post-registration maintenance filing would have been due in 2024–25.
- Other MIRUN uses: Mirun Cosmetic (a contract cosmetics manufacturer), Mirun Tech (auto parts, China), a "MIRUN STUDIO" Facebook page (Cape Town, lists interior design), and a "MIRUN" Amazon storefront whose contents I could not confirm.
- Similar home names: **Miruna House** (home decor, mirunahouse.com), **Mirum** (Mexican home decor and candles), Mirok Home on Amazon. **Miren Home** (mirenhome.com, described as a bedding brand) showed up once and could not be confirmed on a second search.

**Meaning:** built on Slavic *mir* = peace. *Miran* means "calm" in BCS. I found no negative meaning in Serbian, though "Mirun" is not a dictionary word and could read like a nickname (unverified). English speakers will pronounce it inconsistently.

### 7. Domna: the cleanest result, but not clean

**Found:**
- I found **no home-decor or furniture retailer called "Domna"** in the EU, US or Balkans, and no exact Justia trademark record. Neither result is conclusive.
- **Domna** is a UK home-retrofit company (domna.homes; Domna Group, CEO Anna Moore). It was founded in 2023 and reported up to £70m of investment in 2025. It sells energy-efficiency services, which are different from home goods, but it works in the wider "home" sector.
- **Domna Studios** is holiday accommodation in Lindos, Rhodes (domnastudios.com, domnastudios-lindos.com).
- I found nothing at **domna.com** or **domna.rs**.
- Similar names in home furnishings, each one letter away: **DoMA Home Furnishings** (FL), **DOMA Design** (NL), **DOMA** (Brazilian furniture for the US market), **Doma Blends** (UK home fragrance), **shopdomahome.com ("DOMA: Modern Home Decor")**, and **Domaci** (a US home retailer).
- Similar US marks: **DOMINA** (Ponzini brushes).

**Meanings (found):**
- Late Latin and Old Occitan *domna* = "lady".
- Russian **домна = blast furnace**.
- In historical BCS usage, *domna*/*dumna* was a title for women in monasteries; in Dubrovnik *dumna* meant a nun.

**Uncertain:** whether Russian speakers would actually make the "blast furnace" link. Montenegro has a sizeable Russian-speaking community. That is general knowledge, not something this search established.

**Pronunciation:** "DOM-nah" is easy and nearly the same in English and Serbian. In Serbian it clearly echoes ***dom*** = home.

## Recommendation: **Domna**, trading as **"Domna Living"** (domain idea: domnaliving.com / domna.rs)

**Why Domna:**
1. **It is the only candidate with no direct conflict found in home goods.** No home retailer and no exact trademark turned up. Every other candidate had at least one same-category business or trademark (Kutak in Belgrade, MIRUN in Class 21, Stilhaus, Ivora Lights, Oak and Linden, Oaklume on Etsy and in lighting).
2. **The meaning works in both languages.** *Dom* means home in Serbian and other Slavic languages, and Latin *domus* means house. That gives you a short "home" story for EN and SR.
3. **It is easy to say** in English and Serbian, short, and fits a minimal wordmark and logo.
4. **Why "Living" rather than "Home" or "Studio":**
   - "Domna Home" sits close to "DOMA Home" / shopdomahome.com, which sell the same kind of goods.
   - "Domna Studio(s)" collides with the Rhodes accommodation.
   - "Domna Living" returned no results. Whichever you choose, the descriptor has little weight in a trademark comparison; DOMNA is the part that counts.
5. **The cosy-corner idea can stay in the copy** without using Kutak as the brand. EN: *"Make every corner feel like home."* SR: something like *"Svaki kutak — dom."* Use "kutak" only as an ordinary word in a sentence, never styled as a mark. Nomen's 20-year-old Kutak brand sells the same goods in Belgrade.

**Availability is not guaranteed.** I checked no domain, trademark register, company register or social handle directly.

## Remaining risks for Domna Living

- **Confusion with DOMA names:** DOMNA and DOMA differ by one letter, and several DOMA brands sell home furnishings or home fragrance (US, NL, UK). An examiner or an existing owner could treat them as confusingly similar.
- **The UK Domna company:** a well-funded "Domna" in the wider home sector (retrofit services). It may hold or seek UK or EU marks, and may object or confuse UK customers.
- **Russian "blast furnace" meaning:** an industrial association for Russian speakers (relevant in Montenegro).
- **Slight feminine reading:** the Latin/Occitan "lady" meaning and the *-a* ending. Most customers won't notice, but it is not perfectly neutral.
- **Unindexed rights:** Serbian, Montenegrin or BiH national marks, EU marks, or small Instagram or Etsy sellers may not show up in web search.
- **Domains:** domna.com and domna.rs showed no content in search results. That does not mean they are unregistered.
- **SEO:** "Domna" is also a Greek first name and has historical figures (e.g. Domna Yuferova), so brand search results will be mixed early on.

**Fallback if Domna fails clearance:** I would not move to any other candidate on this list as-is. Each has a concrete conflict in home goods or a home-goods trademark class. Screen fresh names instead.

## Checks to run before committing

1. **Trademarks:**
   - Search TMview and EUIPO eSearch, WIPO Global Brand Database, USPTO Trademark Search, the Serbian IP Office (Zavod za intelektualnu svojinu), the Montenegro IP office and the BiH Institute for Intellectual Property.
   - Search for DOMNA and its look-alikes (DOMA, DOMINA, DOMNA LIVING).
   - Nice classes to cover: 35 (retail), 20 (furniture), 21 (housewares), 24 (textiles), 27 (rugs), 11 (lighting), 4 (candles).
2. **Domains and handles:** check .com, .rs (RNIDS), .me, .ba and .eu with a registrar, plus Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest.
3. **Company registers:** Serbian APR, Montenegro CRPS, BiH registers, UK Companies House.
4. **Language check:** ask a few native speakers of Russian and Montenegrin, and Serbian speakers in Vojvodina, how "Domna" lands.
5. **Get a trademark attorney's clearance opinion** before printing packaging or buying ads.
